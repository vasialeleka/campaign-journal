Privacy Policy

My Campaign Journal

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version

My Campaign Journal collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and the app makes no network connections at all. Everything you write stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Your games, handwritten notes, drawings, bookmarks, imported images and character sheet PDFs are saved inside the app's own storage on your iPad or iPhone. Nobody else can read them, including the developer.

They are included in your device backups if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by this app. Files you export or share yourself leave the app at your choosing and go wherever you send them.

Deleting the app deletes its data with it. Export anything you want to keep first.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or email address is ever asked for.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage statistics are collected.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no profiling.
  • No data is sold, shared or sent to any third party.
  • The app opens no network connections, so nothing can leave your device through it.

The contact form on this website

The one place where data is handled is the form on the support page, which is part of this website rather than the app. When you use it, the name, email address and message you type are delivered to the developer by Web3Forms, who pass the message on and do not keep it. Those details are used to answer you and for nothing else: no mailing list, no sharing with anyone. Write to the developer at that form if you want your message deleted after it has been answered.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log requests for security and operational purposes, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Because no personal data reaches the developer through the app, there is nothing to access, correct, export or erase on your behalf. The data in the app is already yours, on your own device, under your control. For anything you sent through the contact form, ask and it will be deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on the App Store.

Contact

Questions about this policy go through the support page.

My Campaign Journal is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any game publisher.

My Campaign Journal

Support and privacy

Get in touch

Questions, bugs or ideas are welcome. Please say which iPad or iPhone you use and which version of the app (Settings → My Campaign Journal), it makes bugs much easier to find.

Common questions

  • Where are my notes stored? On your device only, inside the app. They are included in your device backup if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer.
  • Can I write without an Apple Pencil? Yes. Open the ••• menu and turn on Draw with Finger.
  • How do I add a character sheet? Open the character menu in the top bar, choose New Character, and import any PDF.
  • Does it need an internet connection? No. The app makes no network requests at all.

Privacy Policy

My Campaign Journal does not collect, store or share any personal data.

All notes, drawings, images and PDF files you create or import stay on your device (and in your device backups, if you use them). The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections. Nothing you write is sent anywhere.

Because no data leaves your device, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. Removing the app removes its data with it, so export anything you want to keep first.

The form on this page is the one exception, and it is part of this website rather than the app: the name, email address and message you type are delivered to the developer by Web3Forms. They are used to answer you and nothing else — no mailing list, no sharing with anyone.

My Campaign Journal is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any game publisher.

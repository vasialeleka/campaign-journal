Support and privacy

Get in touch

Questions, bugs or ideas are welcome. Please say which iPad or iPhone you use and which version of the app (Settings → My Campaign Journal), it makes bugs much easier to find.

Common questions

  • Where are my notes stored? On your device only, inside the app. They are included in your device backup if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer.
  • Can I write without an Apple Pencil? Yes. Open the ••• menu and turn on Draw with Finger.
  • How do I add a character sheet? Open the character menu in the top bar, choose New Character, and import any PDF.
  • Does it need an internet connection? No. The app makes no network requests at all.

Privacy

My Campaign Journal collects nothing: no account, no analytics, no advertising, no network connections. Your notes, drawings and PDFs stay on your device. The form above is the one exception, and it belongs to this website rather than the app — what you type there is delivered to the developer so your message can be answered.

The full Privacy Policy spells all of that out.
